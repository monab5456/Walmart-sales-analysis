# Walmart-sales-analysis
The project analyzed 10k rows walmart dataset to invistigate sales data, analyze branch performance, key products,  profitability, sales quantity, growth/decline rate and customer behavior &amp; satisfaction for sales strategy enhancement.

------

## Introduction
  - Retail performance is not just about how much business sells but it's more about understanding sales pattern,profitability matrix, customer
behavior and where performance starts to weaken. In this  end to end data analysis workflow project which starts from python for data
preprocessing followed by Postgres sql and Power BI to turn transaction level data into actionable business insights. The premier goal is to
create potential optimization and enhancements sales strategies.

------------

## Project Objective
In this project, the objective is to deep dive into the walmart dataset to features it's 
  - Performance of key Branches Products.
  - Yearly growth rate.
  - Analyzing revenue and order volume trend across various category and payment method preferences.
  - Time analysis and customer satisfaction.

 to gain insights into potential optimization and enhancements for sales strategies.

## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the insights a business can do 
  - Leverage insights from product line analysis to create targeted marketing campaigns & promotions for least performing categories.
  - Branch specific strategies are required based on the insights to address the unique demand through loyalty program for older customer and new exciting offers for the new customers or incoming customers.
  - capitalize seasonal trends especially during peak months to maximize the sales and customer acquisation through seasonal promotions and advertising.
  - Optimization of stuff,supporting personals and resources for each branch during peak times will enhance the customer experience.

  - Prioritize declining branches by diagnosing whether the revenue loss is driven by lower transaction volume or lower revenue per transaction. Branches with stable transaction volume but sharp revenue declines should receive immediate category, pricing and product-mix analysis, while branches experiencing declines in both revenue and transactions should focus on demand generation and customer retention.
  - Prioritize declining branches by identifying the revenue loss pattern whether it's driven by low sales volume or low revenue per transaction. The category, pricing, discount and product mix analysis insights should by used to improve their growth rate.
